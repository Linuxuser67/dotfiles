# Minimalistic Desktop Wallpapers

A curated collection of clean, simple, and elegant minimalistic wallpapers for your desktop. Perfect for users who appreciate calm, distraction-free visuals that enhance focus and aesthetics.

## 📁 Contents
This repository includes high-resolution wallpapers in various color schemes and styles, all designed with minimalism in mind.

- Solid colors with subtle gradients
- Geometric shapes and lines
- Nature-inspired minimal designs
- Dark and light mode options

## 🖼️ Usage
1. Browse the repository.
2. Click on any image to view it in full size.
3. Right-click → **Save image as...** to download.
4. Set it as your desktop background!

> 💡 Tip: For best results, choose a resolution that matches your screen size.

## 📥 Contributing
Want to add your own minimalistic wallpaper?  
Feel free to open a **Pull Request**! Just make sure your submission follows these guidelines:
- High resolution (at least 1920×1080)
- No logos, watermarks, or copyright content
- Truly minimalistic in design
